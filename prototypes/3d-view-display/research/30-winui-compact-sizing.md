# Syncfusion: Compact sizing support for WinUI controls (Microsoft's Standard and Compact sizing)

https://help.syncfusion.com/winui/common/compact-sizing

> Standard - to accommodate both touch and pointer input.

> Compact - designed primarily to accommodate pointer input.

> Compact sizing reduces the spacing and padding of controls to enable dense, information-rich layouts.

Microsoft's own page (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/design/style/spacing) now redirects to "Content layout and spacing", which no longer carries the passage; a search of it returned the sentence "Compact sizing is designed primarily to accommodate pointer input" and the 32 × 32 epx target of Compact against Standard's 40 × 40 epx, which could not be read on the page itself.
