# Nielsen Norman Group: Toggle-Switch Guidelines

https://www.nngroup.com/articles/toggle-switch-guidelines/

> Toggle switches are best used for changing the state of system functionalities and preferences.

> Toggle switches should take immediate effect

> Separate controls that produce instant results from those that require clicking a command button.

The article's comparison table gives the moment a checkbox's choice applies as:

> After a user clicks a submit button

and a toggle's as:

> Immediately

> If immediate results are not achievable or seem ill-suited, an alternative

(the alternative named being radio buttons or a single checkbox).
