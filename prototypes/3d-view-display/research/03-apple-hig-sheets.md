# Apple Human Interface Guidelines: Sheets

https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sheets (text read from https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/data/design/human-interface-guidelines/sheets.json, the page's own data)

> In iOS and iPadOS, a sheet can be either modal or *nonmodal*.

> When a nonmodal sheet is onscreen, people use its functionality to affect the parent view without dismissing the sheet.

> Use a nonmodal view when you want to present supplementary items that affect the main task in the parent view.

> Sheets resize according to their *detents*, which are particular heights at which a sheet naturally rests.

> The *Cancel* (or Close) button dismisses a sheet without saving any changes. This type of button is common in most sheets.

> **Support swiping to dismiss a sheet.** People expect to swipe vertically to dismiss a sheet instead of tapping a dismiss button.
