# Apple Human Interface Guidelines: Segmented controls

https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/segmented-controls (text read from https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/data/design/human-interface-guidelines/segmented-controls.json, the page's own data)

> Use a segmented control to provide closely related choices that affect an object, state, or view.

> Limit the number of segments in a control.

> Aim for no more than about five to seven segments in a wide interface and no more than about five segments on iPhone.

> Prefer using either text or images — not a mix of both — in a single segmented control.

> Use nouns or noun phrases for segment labels.
