# Onshape help: Isolate

https://cad.onshape.com/help/Content/isolatingparts.htm

> Isolate keeps all selected Part Studio or Assembly entities opaque, while making other entities transparent.

https://cad.onshape.com/help/Content/isolate_a.htm

> Check Select transparent geometry to select, measure, analyze, and edit transparent geometry in the graphics area.

> When unchecked, only non-transparent geometry can be selected, measured, analyzed, and edited.

> Close the dialog or press Escape to exit.
