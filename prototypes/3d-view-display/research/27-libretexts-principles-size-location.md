The two kinds of dimension:

> Size dimensions are necessary so that the material size of the object can be determined.

> Location dimensions are necessary so that parts, holes, or other features can be positioned in or on the object.

Placement:

> Keep the smallest dimensions nearest to the object outline.

> Try to group related dimensions rather than scattering them.

> Try to keep dimensions off the views themselves.

Repetition:

> no dimension should be repeated twice on any sketch or drawing.

Identical features are dimensioned once with their count, as in its example "ø20 – 2 holes".
