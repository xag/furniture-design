# Google Maps Help: Change map details and layers (Android)

https://support.google.com/maps/answer/3092439?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid

> At the top right, tap Layers

> Tap the Map type that you want to get.

> Tap the Map details that you want to include:

(The details listed: Transit, Traffic, Bicycling, Street View, Raised buildings, Air quality.)
