# Fine Woodworking: More quick dimensioning tips (SketchUp's expert dimension settings)

https://finewoodworking.com/2009/02/20/more-quick-dimensioning-tips

SketchUp's own help page for the dialog could not be fetched (its help host did not resolve); this is a woodworking magazine's walkthrough of the same settings.

Hide When Too Small:

> Hide dimensions for small details when you are looking at a larger picture of the model.

> Go to Window>Model Info>Dimensions and click on the Expert Dimension Setting button.

> You can adjust the slider to adjust how small the dimensions need to be before they are hidden.

Hide When Foreshortened:

> Hide dimensions when looking at them from the end.

> Adjust the slider to control how foreshortened the dimension must be before it is hidden.
