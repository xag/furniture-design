# LibreTexts (Northeast Wisconsin Technical College), Technical Sketching: 7.03 Dimensioning

https://workforce.libretexts.org/Courses/Northeast_Wisconsin_Technical_College/Technical_Sketching_(NWTC)/07%3A_Dimensioning/7.03%3A_Dimensioning

> Usually, the dimension line meets the extension line at right angles.

> There should be a gap of about 1 / 16″ where the extension line would meet the outline of the object

> the extension line should go beyond the outermost arrowhead approximately 1 /8″

> there should be not gaps where extension lines cross.

> place dimensions on the views, in a two or three view drawing, where they will be the most easily understood.

(McGill's Principles of Dimensioning, https://www.mcgill.ca/engineeringdesign/node/639, which gives a 1 mm visible gap, returned a 503 when fetched.)
